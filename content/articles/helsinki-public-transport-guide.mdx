Helsinki's public transport system is efficient, easy to use, and covers almost everything a visitor needs — trams, the metro, buses and the Suomenlinna ferry are all part of the same ticketing system, run by HSL (Helsingin seudun liikenne).

## Understanding HSL Zones

HSL divides the region into zones, labelled A through D radiating out from the centre. For most visitors, the **AB zone** — covering central Helsinki, its inner neighborhoods, and Suomenlinna — is all you need. If you're planning a trip further out, such as to Nuuksio National Park or Helsinki-Vantaa Airport, check the specific zone required before buying a ticket.

## Ticket Types

- **Single ticket** — valid for one journey with transfers within a set time window (typically 80 minutes in the AB zone).
- **24-hour, 48-hour and longer passes** — better value if you're using trams or the metro more than twice a day, and usually the most practical option for a multi-day city visit.
- **The HSL app** — lets you buy, store and validate tickets directly on your phone, and is generally the easiest option for visitors rather than dealing with ticket machines or paper tickets.

## Trams, Metro and Buses

**Trams** cover the city centre densely and are often the fastest and most scenic way to get around — tram routes 2 and 3 in particular pass many major sights. The **metro** runs mainly east-west and is most useful for trips to Kamppi, the airport connection, or eastern neighborhoods like Kalasatama. **Buses** fill in the gaps, including routes to the airport and outlying areas like Nuuksio.

## The Suomenlinna Ferry

The ferry to Suomenlinna is included in a standard AB-zone ticket — no separate purchase needed. It departs from Market Square roughly every 20–40 minutes depending on the season and takes about 15 minutes each way.

## Practical Tips for Visitors

- Download the **HSL app** before your trip and set it up with a payment method — it's the smoothest way to handle tickets for a multi-day visit.
- A **multi-day pass** almost always pays for itself if you're combining Suomenlinna, the Design District, and any neighborhood further than a 20-minute walk from your hotel.
- Validate single tickets when boarding if using paper or card tickets — inspectors do check, and fines for invalid tickets are significant.

## Getting Beyond the AB Zone

For day trips like Nuuksio National Park, you'll need a wider zone ticket or a combined train-and-bus ticket — see our [guide to visiting Nuuksio from Helsinki](/blog/how-to-visit-nuuksio-national-park-from-helsinki) for the specific route and ticketing details.
