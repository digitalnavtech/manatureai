Porvoo is the most popular day trip from Helsinki, and for good reason — it's close, easy to reach without a car, and delivers a genuinely different atmosphere from the capital within an hour. This guide covers exactly how to plan the day.

## Getting There

The most straightforward option is the **direct bus from Helsinki's Kamppi bus terminal**, which runs frequently throughout the day and takes 50–60 minutes. Buy tickets in advance online or on the day at the terminal. If you're driving, the trip takes about 45 minutes via the coastal or motorway route, and parking is available near the Old Town.

For a different kind of day, the **J.L. Runeberg steamship** runs from Helsinki's Market Square in summer, taking about 3.5 hours each way — worth it only if the boat journey itself is part of the appeal rather than a means to an end.

## What to See in a Half-Day

Once in Porvoo, the **Old Town (Vanha Porvoo)** is entirely walkable. Start at the **cathedral** on the hill, then wander down through the cobbled streets toward the **riverside warehouses** — the most photographed view in town, especially in late-afternoon light. Along the way, look out for small artisan workshops and galleries tucked into the wooden buildings.

## Where to Eat

Riverside restaurants serve Baltic fish and seasonal Finnish menus with outdoor seating in summer. For something distinctly local, stop at a bakery for a **Runeberg torte** — a small almond-and-rum cake associated with Finland's national poet, J.L. Runeberg, who lived in Porvoo. The Old Town's cafés are well suited to a slower coffee break between sightseeing stops.

## A Sample Day Trip Schedule

- **9:00** — Depart Helsinki by bus
- **10:00** — Arrive Porvoo, walk to the cathedral and Old Town
- **12:00** — Lunch by the river
- **13:30** — Riverside warehouses, artisan shops, bakery stop
- **15:30** — Free time or a walk along the river south of the Old Town
- **17:00** — Return bus to Helsinki
