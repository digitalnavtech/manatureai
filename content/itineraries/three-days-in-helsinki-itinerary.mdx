This itinerary is built for a first-time visitor who wants to see Helsinki's essentials without rushing, plus one day outside the city to get a taste of Finnish nature or small-town charm.

## Day 1: City Centre and Design

Morning at **Senate Square** and Helsinki Cathedral, then down to **Market Square** for coffee. Spend the rest of the morning and early afternoon in the **Design District**. Lunch at the **Old Market Hall**. Evening: a **public sauna** session — Löyly or Allas Sea Pool for a modern setting, Kotiharjun Sauna for tradition.

## Day 2: Suomenlinna and Neighborhoods

Mid-morning **ferry to Suomenlinna** from Market Square. Spend three to four hours on the island — fortress walls, a museum, and lunch or a picnic on the ramparts. Afternoon ferry back, then explore **Kallio** or **Töölö** for the rest of the day.

## Day 3: Day Trip Out of the City

Choose **Porvoo** (50–60 minutes by bus) for Old Town charm, or **Nuuksio National Park** (about an hour by train and bus) for forest and lake nature. Either works as a half-day or full day, depending on your departure time.

## Where This Itinerary Can Flex

If the weather turns on your planned outdoor day, swap it with day 1's more indoor-friendly plan.
