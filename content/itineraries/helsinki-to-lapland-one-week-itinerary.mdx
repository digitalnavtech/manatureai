This route combines Helsinki's compact city appeal with Finnish Lapland's Arctic scale — a good structure if you only have one trip to Finland and want to see both sides of the country.

## Days 1–3: Helsinki

Follow our [3-day Helsinki itinerary](/itineraries/three-days-in-helsinki-itinerary): city centre and Design District on day one, Suomenlinna and neighborhoods on day two, and a half-day trip to Porvoo or Nuuksio on day three. Use the evening of day three to pack for colder weather ahead.

## Day 4: Fly North

Take a direct flight from Helsinki to Rovaniemi, Ivalo or Kittilä (around 1.5 hours) and settle into your Lapland base. Use the rest of the day for a short orientation walk or a low-key activity like a sauna, since flights and transfers can eat up much of the day.

## Day 5: Snow Activities

Spend the day on a **husky or reindeer safari**, or on the slopes if you're near a ski resort like Levi or Ylläs. In summer, substitute a fell hiking day instead.

## Day 6: Aurora Chasing and Rovaniemi

If visiting in aurora season (late September–March), join a guided **northern lights chasing tour** in the evening, timed around clear-sky forecasts. During the day, visit **Rovaniemi** and the Arctic Circle line if you haven't already, including the Santa Claus Village if traveling with kids.

## Day 7: Return Flight

Fly back to Helsinki, with enough buffer time built in for a same-day international connection or one final evening in the capital before departing.

## Adjusting This Itinerary

This plan assumes winter/aurora season, which is the most popular time to combine both regions. For a summer version, replace aurora chasing and husky safaris with midnight-sun hiking on the fells, and expect a very different but equally rewarding week.
