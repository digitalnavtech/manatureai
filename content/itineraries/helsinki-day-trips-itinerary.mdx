If you're based in Helsinki for several days, day trips are the easiest way to see more of Finland without the logistics of a multi-city itinerary. Here's how the main options compare.

## Suomenlinna — Half a Day

The shortest and easiest option: a 15-minute ferry from Market Square, included in a standard transport ticket. Three to four hours covers the fortress walls and a museum or two. Read the full [Suomenlinna guide](/destinations/suomenlinna).

## Porvoo — Half to Full Day

A 50–60 minute bus ride each way, with 4–6 hours in the Old Town giving a comfortable, unhurried visit. The most popular day trip from Helsinki for a reason — see the [Helsinki to Porvoo day trip guide](/blog/helsinki-to-porvoo-day-trip).

## Nuuksio National Park — Half to Full Day

About an hour each way by train and bus (or a direct seasonal summer bus). Best for travelers who want genuine forest and lake nature rather than another town. See the [guide to visiting Nuuksio from Helsinki](/blog/how-to-visit-nuuksio-national-park-from-helsinki).

## Turku — Full Day

The longest of these options at about two hours each way by train, but it delivers a genuinely different city with a medieval castle, cathedral and archipelago access. Best attempted as a dedicated full day rather than combined with anything else. Read the full [Turku destination guide](/destinations/turku).

## Combining Two Day Trips in One Visit

If your Helsinki stay is four days or longer, Suomenlinna pairs easily with almost any other day, since it only takes half a day. Porvoo and Nuuksio are each better as their own full or half day rather than combined together, given the travel time on each end.

## Choosing Based on Your Priorities

- **Want the easiest, shortest trip?** Suomenlinna.
- **Want charming architecture and an easy pace?** Porvoo.
- **Want real forest and nature?** Nuuksio National Park.
- **Want a second full city with archipelago access?** Turku.
